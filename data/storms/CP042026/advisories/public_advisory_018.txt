DISCUSSION AND OUTLOOK
----------------------
At 500 PM HST (0300 UTC), the center of Tropical Storm Pena was 
located near latitude 10.5 North, longitude 166.0 West. Pena is 
moving toward the west near 8 mph (13 km/h). A turn to the 
west-northwest, along with an increase in forward speed is expected 
to occur over the next few days. 
 
Maximum sustained winds have increased to near 45 mph (75 km/h) with 
higher gusts. Slight strengthening is possible over the next 24 
hours, followed by gradual weakening thereafter. 
 
Tropical-storm-force winds extend outward up to 105 miles (165 km)
from the center.
 
The estimated minimum central pressure is 1002 mb (29.59 inches).
