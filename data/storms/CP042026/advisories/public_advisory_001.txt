DISCUSSION AND OUTLOOK
----------------------
At 1100 AM HST (2100 UTC), the center of Tropical Storm Pena was
located near latitude 11.3 North, longitude 150.1 West. Pena is
moving toward the west near 12 mph (19 km/h), and this motion is
expected to prevail through the next several days.
 
Satellite data indicate that the maximum sustained winds are near 
45 mph (75 km/h) with higher gusts.  Gradual strengthening is 
expected over the next day or so, followed by no significant change 
through early next week.
 
Tropical-storm-force winds extend outward up to 45 miles (75 km)
from the center.
 
The estimated minimum central pressure is 1002 mb (29.59 inches).
