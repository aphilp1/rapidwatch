DISCUSSION AND OUTLOOK
----------------------
At 500 AM HST (1500 UTC), the center of Tropical Storm Pena was
located near latitude 9.9 North, longitude 167.7 West. Pena is
moving toward the west-southwest near 9 mph (15 km/h), and this
general motion is expected for the next few days.
 
Maximum sustained winds are near 45 mph (75 km/h) with higher gusts. 
Little change in strength is forecast during the next day or so, 
followed by gradual weakening after Thursday.
 
Tropical-storm-force winds extend outward up to 115 miles (185 km)
from the center.
 
The estimated minimum central pressure is 1002 mb (29.59 inches).
