DISCUSSION AND OUTLOOK
----------------------
At 1100 PM HST (0900 UTC), the center of Tropical Storm Pena was 
located near latitude 11.5 North, longitude 155.0 West. Pena is 
moving toward the west near 7 mph (11 km/h) and a general westward 
motion is expected to continue over the next few days.
 
Satellite wind data indicates that maximum sustained winds have 
decreased to 45 mph (75 km/h) with higher gusts. Some additional 
weakening is possible today followed by gradual strengthening. 
 
Tropical-storm-force winds extend outward up to 35 miles (55 km)
from the center.
 
The estimated minimum central pressure is 1002 mb (29.59 inches).
