01 · THE MODELWhat NHC runs, and what we rebuilt

Rapid intensification (RI) has an official definition: a storm's peak winds rising by 30 knots or more within 24 hours — roughly a jump of two Saffir-Simpson categories. It happens in only about 1 in 15 forecast windows, but it's behind nearly every catastrophic surprise the Gulf has produced.

NHC's workhorse guidance for it is the SHIPS Rapid Intensification Index (SHIPS-RII): a statistical model that, every six hours, reads a handful of measurements about the storm's environment and outputs one number — the probability that RI begins in the next 24 hours. We rebuilt that model from the same public developmental dataset NHC's own version was fitted on, in both its classic forms (discriminant analysis and logistic regression; the logistic one is shown here).

Scoring is leave-one-storm-out: to score Katrina, the model is retrained on the other 537 storms and has never seen Katrina. Every probability on this page was produced that way — no storm ever grades its own homework.

04 · TRUSTWhen it says 25%, does 25% happen?

A probability forecast is honest if events happen as often as it claims. That's the reliability test below — and this model passes it cleanly in every well-populated bin.

The trade-off NHC forecasters live with daily: a low alert bar catches most RI events but cries wolf four times in five; a high bar almost never fires. This is why RI guidance is issued as probabilities, not yes/no alarms — and the published SHIPS-RII has the same character.

06 · NEXTThe Gulf gap, and the ocean-heat idea

Across every threshold tested, this model is weakest exactly where RI is most common: Gulf skill +5.7% vs +9.4% basin-wide, while the Gulf's RI rate runs double the Atlantic's. That gap is the project's target. The first enhancement experiment — sampling measured warm-water depth ahead of the storm instead of under it, per the RapidWatch §5.7 finding — has already reproduced its physical signal decisively (ahead-of-track D26 separates RI from non-RI at p ≈ 10−17) and nudged Gulf skill in the predicted direction; a larger test sample is queued to decide whether the improvement is real.
